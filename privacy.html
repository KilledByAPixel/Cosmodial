Privacy Policy

Cosmodial Sky Atlas · Last updated July 8, 2026

The short version: Cosmodial collects nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. Everything the app does runs on your own device.

Your location

When you tap “use my location,” your device shares your latitude and longitude with the app so it can draw the sky correctly for where you are. That location is used only on your device, in the moment, to compute what’s overhead. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and never stored off your device. You can also just type a location instead — the app never needs your device’s location service at all.

Data stored on your device

To work offline and remember your preferences, Cosmodial saves a few things in your browser’s local storage — your chosen location, your favorites, display settings, and a cached copy of the star catalog and recent satellite orbit data. This all stays on your device. It is never sent to us. Clearing the site’s data in your browser, or uninstalling the app, removes it.

Network requests

Cosmodial is offline-first, but it makes one kind of outbound request: downloading public satellite orbit data (for the ISS and Tiangong) from third-party space-tracking services such as CelesTrak and Where the ISS at?. These requests contain no personal data — no location, no identity. As with any request on the internet, the service you download from receives your device’s IP address as a normal part of delivering the response; that is standard internet operation and is not something Cosmodial collects or controls.

No analytics, ads, or cookies

Cosmodial contains no advertising, no analytics or usage tracking, no third-party trackers, and no cookies.

Children

Cosmodial is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the “last updated” date above will change with it.
